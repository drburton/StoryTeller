# Trademark Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

StoryTeller's code is free to use under the MIT license (see `LICENSE`). The MIT license covers the code. It gives no rights to the StoryTeller name or logo. This policy explains how you may use them, so that people can always tell an official StoryTeller release from a modified or unrelated one.

"The marks" below means the name "StoryTeller" when used for this visual novel plugin, and the StoryTeller logo (`docs/images/icon.png`).

## Uses that need no permission

- **Saying what your game uses.** "Made with StoryTeller", "Uses the StoryTeller plugin", or a credit line in your game, store page, or website.
- **Talking about the project.** Articles, videos, tutorials, courses, reviews, and talks about StoryTeller.
- **Naming add-ons and tools truthfully.** "Character pack for StoryTeller" or "TaleScript syntax highlighting", as long as the name makes clear the product is your own and works with StoryTeller.
- **Distributing unchanged copies.** Sharing the official plugin as released, with its name and logo.
- **Using the logo to link to the project,** unchanged and at a reasonable size.

## Uses that need permission

- Naming your own product, plugin, engine, fork, company, or domain "StoryTeller" or something close to it.
- Distributing a **modified** version of the plugin under the StoryTeller name or logo. Forks are welcome under the MIT license; give yours a different name and say that it is based on StoryTeller.
- Changing the logo, or combining it with your own marks.
- Suggesting that the project made, endorses, sponsors, or certifies your product, course, or service.
- Selling merchandise with the marks.

To ask for permission, open an issue in the repository: https://github.com/drburton/StoryTeller/issues
